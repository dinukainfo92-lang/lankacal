Frequently asked questions

What is EMI?
EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is the fixed amount you pay every month toward a loan. Each EMI first covers interest on the outstanding balance, and the remainder reduces the principal — as the balance shrinks, more of each payment goes toward principal.
How is EMI calculated on a reducing balance loan?
This calculator uses the standard reducing-balance method: EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of monthly installments.
How do extra monthly payments affect my loan?
Any amount paid above the EMI is applied straight to the outstanding principal in that same month. This shortens the loan term and cuts total interest paid, without needing to refinance.
Does this calculator include bank fees or charges?
No. Processing fees, loan insurance, valuation costs, rate changes on variable-rate loans, and early-settlement charges aren't included in this estimate — confirm the full cost with your lender.
