About Lankacal

Lankacal is a free calculator toolkit built specifically for people living and working in Sri Lanka. It started with two everyday problems: figuring out exactly how much income tax (APIT) comes out of a monthly salary, and working out a loan's real monthly EMI before signing anything.

Every tool runs entirely in your browser — your numbers are never uploaded or stored anywhere. More Sri Lanka-specific calculators are being added regularly; if there's a tool you'd like to see, it's on the roadmap above.
