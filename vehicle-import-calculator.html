How this is calculated.
CIF → + Customs Import Duty (30%) → + Ports & Airports Levy (10%) → + Excise Duty (by engine cc, or kW for EVs) → + Luxury Tax (on the CIF amount above Rs. 5.0M–6.0M, depending on fuel type) → + SSCL (2.5% on the running total) → + VAT (18% on the running total) → + a fixed Rs. 15,000 Vehicle Entitlement Levy (VEL).

Frequently asked questions

What taxes apply when importing a vehicle to Sri Lanka?
Customs Import Duty (CID), Excise Duty, Ports and Airports Levy (PAL), Luxury Tax (above certain CIF thresholds), the Social Security Contribution Levy (SSCL), Value Added Tax (VAT), and a fixed Vehicle Entitlement Levy (VEL) — each calculated in sequence on top of the running total.
What is CIF value?
Cost, Insurance and Freight — the vehicle's purchase price plus international insurance and shipping to a Sri Lankan port. Every import duty and tax is calculated from this CIF value.
Can I import a used vehicle to Sri Lanka?
Generally only vehicles up to around 3 years from their date of manufacture, with limited exceptions. Confirm current eligibility with Sri Lanka Customs or a licensed clearing agent before committing to an import.
How accurate is this calculator?
It's an estimate built from publicly available rate information, cross-checked across several sources. Rates change often via Customs gazette notifications — always confirm your final figure with a licensed clearing agent before importing.
