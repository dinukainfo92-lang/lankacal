Frequently asked questions

What is APIT in Sri Lanka?
APIT (Advance Personal Income Tax), also known as PAYE, is the tax an employer deducts each month from an employee's regular profits from employment and remits to the Inland Revenue Department on the employee's behalf.
How much of my monthly salary is tax-free?
Effective from 01.04.2025, the first Rs. 150,000 of monthly employment income (Rs. 1,800,000 a year) is tax-free personal relief. Only the balance above that is taxed in bands from 6% to 36%.
Is EPF calculated before or after tax?
APIT is calculated on gross salary before the 8% employee EPF contribution is deducted. The EPF amount still leaves your pay packet each month, but it's banked in your name, and the employer separately contributes EPF 12% and ETF 3% on top at no cost to you.
Does this calculator cover bonuses or gratuity?
No. Bonuses, gratuity, and other lump-sum payments are taxed separately under IRD Table 02 and aren't part of this monthly APIT estimate.
